Background · Focus

Michael
Ikirodah

I design and build financial systems that record and move money reliably. My work covers payment processing, accounting models, and systems that must handle failures without losing or duplicating money.

Engineering Focus

Building financial software requires more care than building an ordinary app. When money moves, showing success too early or hiding a failed update can lead to serious errors.

I design clear boundaries around money movement: permanent records of every transaction, separate accounts for money waiting to settle, and independent parts that keep working when an external service fails.

Architectural Principles

PRIN.01

Rules Over Heuristics

Keep balances correct and prevent duplicate transactions at the database and system boundaries, rather than relying only on application code.

PRIN.02

Out-of-Band Work

Run heavy verification and risk checks outside the payment flow. When a payment is ready to run, it should make a fast, predictable decision from already-prepared information.

PRIN.03

Blast Containment

Keep responsibilities separate. For example, store identity data in a dedicated system and hold incoming payments in dedicated accounts so one failure cannot spread across the system.
